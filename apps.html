Sticker Tracker

This app is to be able to track the collected and missing sticker from the 2026 World Cup Album. You can click to mark a sticker as collected/missing and you can also sort the countries by alphabetical order, completion order and orignial album order. If you wish to share the album with other people or in other devices then you can copy the generated export code and import it from the other device.
